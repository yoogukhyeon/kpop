A first-time dancer, a friend who wants a polished video and someone who trains regularly do not need the same lesson. Choose the result you want before choosing a studio. This guide is researched from supplier information, not a claim that we attended or ranked every class.

## Choose the outcome first

| Your priority | Start with | Check before booking |
|---|---|---|
| Trying choreography for the first time | A visitor-oriented group lesson | The level and language of your specific session |
| A video with your friends | A private lesson or filming option | Whether editing and delivery are included |
| Learning a particular song | A session accepting requests | Whether the song is confirmed, not merely requested |
| More demanding training | An academy's published timetable | Required experience and visitor eligibility |

A product title is not a standard package. Compare the option you will actually buy.

## Compare the final cost, not the headline price

Select the same date and participant count on two candidates. Compare the checkout total, private-session or filming extras, cancellation deadline and the cost of reaching the studio. A low starting price may belong to a different option.

We do not publish a fixed price: currencies, packages and promotions change. If an important inclusion is unclear, ask the seller before paying rather than assuming an attractive thumbnail confirms it.

## Fit the lesson into your day

Allow for travel, the operator's required arrival buffer, changing and a break afterwards. Avoid placing a class beside a full-day tour just because both say Seoul. Keep a Hongdae lesson with Mapo stops and a COEX lesson with Gangnam plans.

The [trip planner](/en#planner) groups nearby places but cannot confirm live studio schedules or a connection to your concert. Use the [group guides](/en/groups) to pick flexible stops around the lesson.

## Six questions to send before payment

1. What is the exact studio address and entrance for my date?
2. What level and teaching language apply to this session?
3. Is our requested song confirmed or only a preference?
4. Are filming, editing and delivery included in this option?
5. Is the minimum class size met, and when is booking confirmed?
6. What happens if the operator changes the session or we arrive late?

Follow the studio's own clothing and shoe instructions. Bring water. If you mainly want a photo, dislike group choreography or have a tightly timed concert day, leaving this slot free may be a better choice than buying another activity.
