Learning a choreography in Seoul is one of the most popular K-pop experiences — and you don't need any dance experience to enjoy it.

## Two kinds of classes

**Classes made for visitors**

- Taught in English (sometimes Japanese or Chinese), beginner-friendly.
- Usually teach one well-known song in 1–2 hours, often with a recorded video at the end.
- Booked online in advance, often through travel platforms.

**Drop-in classes at dance academies**

- Real academy classes alongside local students; instruction is mostly in Korean.
- Faster pace, more advanced choreography.
- Better if you already dance and want an authentic class.

## Before you book

- **Check the song.** Most classes announce the song in advance — pick one you love.
- **Check the level.** "Beginner" at an academy can still be fast.
- **Check what's included:** video recording, changing rooms, private vs group.
- **Group size:** private classes for friends are popular for birthdays and fan trips.

## What to wear and bring

- Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor sneakers.
- A water bottle and a small towel.
- Learn the chorus beforehand if you can — you'll get more out of a short class.

## Fit it into your trip

Classes take a few hours including travel, so they work well on a day without a concert. Use the [trip planner](/en) to find a free day around your events.
