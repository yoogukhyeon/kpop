This is a sample route for a first K-pop trip to Seoul. It groups stops by neighbourhood so you spend your time at fan spots, not on the subway. Swap stops in and out depending on your group.

## Day 1 — Central Seoul and Yongsan

- **Morning:** HiKR Ground in Jung-gu, a free K-pop experience space run by the Korea Tourism Organization.
- **Lunch:** Myeongdong, with its K-pop shops nearby.
- **Afternoon:** Yongsan, for photos outside the HYBE building.
- **Evening:** a walk along the Han River.

## Day 2 — Seongsu and Gangnam

- **Morning:** Seongsu's pop-up stores and cafes — check what's on that week in the [events list](/en/events). SM Entertainment is in this area.
- **Afternoon:** K-Star Road in Apgujeong, with bear statues for many groups.
- **Evening:** Gangnam shopping and dinner.

## Day 3 — Hongdae and Mapo

- **Afternoon:** Hongdae's K-pop shops and birthday cafes. YG Entertainment is in nearby Hapjeong. See our [Hongdae guide](/en/guides/hongdae-kpop-guide).
- **Evening:** street busking in Hongdae.

## Add a music show or concert

- **Music shows** run on weekdays and weekends — see [how to attend](/en/guides/kpop-music-show-guide). They can take most of a day, so drop one neighbourhood if you get in.
- **Concert day:** keep it light. Merch lines start early, and leaving the venue takes time — see [venue tips](/en/guides/seoul-kpop-concert-venues).

## Make it yours

This route is generic. Put your group, bias and dates into the [trip planner](/en) to get a version built around your bias's agency, birthday cafes and the events actually happening while you're in Seoul.
