Most K-pop concerts in Seoul happen at a handful of venues. Getting in is easy; getting out after 15,000 other fans is the hard part.

## KSPO Dome (Olympic Park)

The most-used arena for K-pop concerts, inside Olympic Park in Songpa-gu.

- **Subway:** Olympic Park Station (Line 5 and Line 9).
- **Exit:** Exit 3 or 4. Follow the concert banners — about a 7–10 minute walk.
- **Line 5 tip:** Line 5 splits into two branches in the east. Make sure your train is going towards **Macheon**, not Hanam.
- **After the show:** Olympic Park Station gets packed. Walking to **Hanseong Baekje Station** (Line 9) or **Mongchontoseong Station** (Line 8) is often faster.

Other Olympic Park venues — **Olympic Hall** and **Handball Gymnasium** — use the same stations.

## Gocheok Sky Dome

Seoul's only domed stadium, used for the biggest concerts and award shows.

- **Subway:** Guil Station (Line 1), Exit 2 — a few minutes' walk.
- **From central Seoul:** Line 1 runs directly from Seoul Station.
- **After the show:** Guil Station's gates jam right after the encore. Either wait 20–30 minutes inside, or walk about 15 minutes to **Gaebong Station** (Line 1).

## Inspire Arena (Incheon)

A large arena on Yeongjong Island near Incheon International Airport. It's far from central Seoul, so check the official transport and shuttle information before you go and plan your route home in advance — late-night options are limited.

## Concert day tips

![Beat the crowd after the show](fig:1)

- **Merch lines open hours early.** If you want official merch, arrive in the morning.
- **Coin lockers** at nearby stations fill up fast. Travel light.
- **Pickup booths** for foreigner tickets also have queues — bring your passport.
- **Stay near your venue** if you have back-to-back shows. Jamsil and Olympic Park have plenty of hotels for KSPO Dome nights.
