Hongdae — the area around Hongik University in Mapo-gu — is where young Seoul hangs out, and it's packed with things K-pop fans love.

## Why fans love Hongdae

- **Street busking.** Dancers and singers perform K-pop covers on the street, especially in the evening and on weekends.
- **Birthday cafes.** Mapo-gu, including Hongdae, Sinchon and Hapjeong, has the biggest concentration of fan-run birthday cafes in Seoul. See our [birthday cafe guide](/en/guides/kpop-birthday-cafe-guide).
- **K-pop shops** selling albums, photocards and goods.
- **Neighbours worth combining:** Hapjeong (YG Entertainment's area) and Sangam, home to several broadcasters.

## Getting there

- **Hongik University Station** is served by Seoul Subway Line 2, the Gyeongui–Jungang Line and the **Airport Railroad (AREX)**.
- AREX connects Hongik University Station directly with Incheon International Airport, which makes Hongdae a convenient first or last stop.

## A Hongdae day

![A Hongdae day](fig:1)

1. **Afternoon:** browse the K-pop shops and any birthday cafe open that week.
2. **Early evening:** walk the main streets to catch busking performances.
3. **Night:** street food and cafes — the area stays lively late.

## Staying in Hongdae

Hongdae is a popular base for fans: it's lively, has many budget and mid-range hotels, and the airport train stops right there. If your concerts are in the east of Seoul (Olympic Park), allow extra travel time.
