SideQuest Day is free to use. To keep it that way, some pages contain **affiliate links** to travel services — for example tours and experiences, eSIMs, transport passes and hotels from partners such as Klook, KKday and Agoda.

## What this means for you

- If you click an affiliate link and make a booking, we may earn a small commission from the partner.
- **You pay the same price** whether or not you use our link.
- Affiliate sections are labelled on the page, and links to partners are marked as sponsored for search engines.

## What it doesn't change

- We recommend services because they're useful for K-pop trips, not because of commission rates.
- Our trip plans, event listings and guides are not paid placements.
- We never sell or resell concert tickets, and we don't link to ticket resale services.

If a page ever offers a paid or sponsored placement, it will be clearly marked "Sponsored".
