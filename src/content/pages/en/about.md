SideQuest Day helps international K-pop fans plan a trip to Seoul around the group and member they love — their "bias".

Pick a group, a member and your travel dates, and SideQuest Day lines up what's happening while you're in town: concerts and fan meetings, member birthdays and the birthday cafes fans open around them, pop-up stores, and pilgrimage spots grouped by neighbourhood so you can see more and travel less.

## Who it's for

Fans travelling to Korea, often for the first time, who want to know:

- what's on during their dates,
- how to get tickets or attend music shows as a foreigner,
- and how to visit fan spots respectfully.

SideQuest Day is available in English, Japanese, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish and Korean.

## Where our information comes from

- **Events** come from official notices (agencies, Weverse, ticketing sites) and, for birthday cafes, from the fans who host them. Every event links to its source.
- **Places** such as agency buildings, venues and landmarks are checked against official sources. Items we haven't verified yet are marked on the page.
- **Guides** are written for visitors and list their sources at the bottom.

Event details change often. Always check the official notice before you go.

## Independence

SideQuest Day is a fan-made guide. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any artist, agency, broadcaster or ticketing platform. Artist and group names are used only to describe them. We don't use artist photos.

## How SideQuest Day is funded

SideQuest Day is free to use. Some pages link to travel services such as tours, eSIMs and hotels. Some of these may be affiliate links: if you book through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we recommend. See our [affiliate disclosure](/en/disclosure).
